# Snap simulation

Generated by `tools/snap_sim.py` from the built lid and tray (see its header for the model). Forces are at the front edge. The hinges' spring-back pushes the closed lid's front edge up with 0.1..0.9 N: under 0.9 N the snap "does not hold", under 4 N it is "weak" (the lid comes open when the device is handled). Friction 0.3. Seam = how far the front edge comes up before the snap carries the push.

That push is the hinges' spring-back only. Rims that meet near the hinges lever the lid open with far more (seen on the built PaperDIY version; the lid's underside is relieved there, PART_RELIEF): nothing here covers a lid that is levered open.

Springs (plate model): lip 19.1 N/mm. Drawn: bump 0.45 mm, lip 0.05 mm off the tray; no stops (LATCH['stop'] is None). On the built parts the lip has to bend 0.37 mm to pass.
